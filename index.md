Hello! I'm a PhD student studying electrical engineering at the [University of Colorado Boulder](https://www.colorado.edu). My general interests lie in applied electromagnetics, particularly in antennas, lenses, and numerical electromagnetics.

I received B.S. degrees from CU in applied mathematics and electrical engineering. I'm currently with the [Antenna Research Group](https://www.colorado.edu/lab/antenna/) at CU, where I'm working on the design of lenses for near field manipulation of phased arrays. I previously worked on electrically small antennas, particularly for small arrays, as well as work in the High Speed Digital Engineering Group at CU on high bandwidth conductive ink structures.

In my free time, I enjoy taking [walks in the woods](/hiking.html). 

A copy of my resume is available [here](/resume.html). I can be reached at <a href="mailto: edward@wawrzynek.com">edward@wawrzynek.com</a>.
